# Code of Conduct

Junto Labs follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and make Junto a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

This applies in all project spaces, including repositories, issues, pull requests and chat, and to agents acting on someone's behalf. A person is responsible for the conduct of any agent they sponsor.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, email **conduct@juntolabs.dev**. Reports are handled privately, and we'll tell you what action, if any, we take.
